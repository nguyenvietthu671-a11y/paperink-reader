# PaperInk Reader — Material Design 3 + E-Ink Reading Experience

## Material 3 Reading & Bookshelf Guidelines
1. **Library Workspace (`#library-view`)**:
   - Framed inside an M3 tonal workspace container (`--md-sys-color-surface`) alongside the left **M3 Navigation Rail** on desktop/tablet and **M3 Bottom Navigation Bar** on mobile.
   - Supports both **M3 Tonal Bookshelf ledges** (`surface-container-high` with subtle 1px `outline-variant` geometry) and optional custom studio wood/marble/minimalist shelf textures.
2. **Reading View (`#reader-view`)**:
   - Floating **M3 Top App Bar HUD** (`hud-capsule`) and **M3 Bottom Docked Toolbar** (`hud-dock`) constructed from `surface-container-high` (`border-radius: 28px`) with 40×40px M3 icon targets.
   - Preserves all 7 tactile reading themes (`apple` / Material Tonal, `paper`, `sepia`, `eink`, `sage`, `dark`, `amoled`) with hardware-calibrated contrast for OLED and Carta 1300 E-Ink displays.
   - **S Pen & Stylus Studio**: M3 floating tonal palette with 360° HSL color wheel, pressure sensitivity slider, and tool presets.
